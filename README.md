## 🤔 What is this guy up to?

I live where **AI** meets **security**, which is a fun neighbourhood. Everything is on fire, but in an interesting way.

- 🧠 **Teaching models new tricks**: fine-tuning, evaluation, and keeping them on a leash
- 🛡️ **Making the AI stack harder to break**: sanitising what goes in and out, auditing local model servers
- 🧰 **Building tools that normal humans can use**: click a button, get a result, no PhD required
- 🧪 **Experimenting in public**: some projects ship, some explode, all of them teach me something

## 🧭 My rules of the lab

- 📏 **Measure honestly.** A claim needs a benchmark, and a benchmark needs its caveats written down.
- 🔒 **Secure by default.** Secrets stay on the server, things are private until you say otherwise.
- ✂️ **Keep it small.** Typed code, tests that mean something, docs a stranger can follow.
- 💸 **Free first.** If you need a credit card to try it, I go looking for a free way.
